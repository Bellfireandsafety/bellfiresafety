The Code, In Plain Terms

Why It Matters

§ NFPA 96 — Standard for Ventilation Control and Fire Protection of Commercial Cooking Operations. Requires exhaust systems to be kept free of grease buildup and cleaned on a schedule based on cooking volume, with dated service records kept on file.

This is what grease buildup looks like inside a duct that's gone too long without service — and it's exactly what NFPA 96 requires kitchens to stay ahead of.

In plain terms: grease left in a hood, duct, or fan is one of the leading causes of restaurant fires. A current cleaning record is often what stands between a passed fire marshal inspection and a citation — and between a valid insurance policy and a denied claim.
